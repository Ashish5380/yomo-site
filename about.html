Chat-first · Read-only · Made in India

A money app that talks like a friend and counts like an accountant.

Most money apps give you a dashboard and let you do the thinking. Yomo flips it: ask in plain words — "how much went to food this month?", "can I afford Goa?", "old regime or new?" — and get an answer in rupees that is actually computed from your own records.

It reads the alerts and documents you already receive: Gmail bank alerts, forwarded emails, statements you upload, and on Android the bank SMS on your phone. It never asks for a bank login, never stores an OTP, and cannot move a rupee.

The mission is simple: every Indian should be able to read their own money.

How it is built

The model never does the arithmetic. Every balance, total, percentage and projection is computed in code in integer paise, turned into a rupee string, and handed to the assistant to quote. If a reply needs a number, the number was calculated first — a rule enforced in the system, not asked for in a prompt.

Three voices, one set of facts. Roast tells you the truth sharply, Pro gives you the analysis, Zen gives you calm guardrails. Roast unlocks after your first week, once there is enough real history to be fair about it.

Built in India for Indian money. Salary dates, UPI narrations, 21 banks' alert formats, credit-card cycles, CAS statements, SIPs and two tax regimes are first-class objects — not localisations bolted on later.

Small, deliberate releases. Yomo runs as a private beta. Features land when they are tested against real statements, and the free plan stays useful on purpose.

What Yomo is, and is not

Yomo is a read-only money analytics tool built by Prasthana. It cannot move money, place orders, hold funds or act on your accounts, and it never asks for a bank password or an OTP. Yomo is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser and does not sell investment products; its insights are informational, not personalised advice. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks — read the scheme documents before investing.
