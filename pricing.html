Pricing Questions, Zero Sugarcoat.

Can I cancel anytime?expand_more
Yes, anytime — from the app or by emailing hello@prasthana.in. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid month. Your Rs 99/mo rate is locked for as long as your founding membership stays active; standard pricing applies if you return after cancelling.
Do you offer refunds?expand_more
If the first month is not for you, reply within 7 days of your first payment and we refund it in full — no questions asked. Subsequent months are pro-rata free if we ever take the service down.
How is my data kept secure?expand_more
Sync is strictly read-only via the RBI-regulated Account Aggregator framework and official broker APIs. Data is encrypted with 256-bit AES at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit, and you can revoke consent instantly from your bank app. Full details on the Security page.
Is Yomo a SEBI-registered investment adviser?expand_more
No. Yomo is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser and does not sell, execute or distribute investment products. The Roast, Pro and Zen personas produce informational analytics — not personalized investment advice. Always read scheme-related documents and consult a registered adviser before acting.
