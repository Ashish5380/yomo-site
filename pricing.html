Billing questions

How is payment taken during the beta?

By hand, on purpose. You request a plan from the app or by email, we confirm the price in writing and activate it. No card is stored by Yomo, and there is no auto-renewal you cannot see.

What happens if I stop paying?

The account drops to Free at the end of the period. Nothing is deleted, and exporting or deleting your data stays available.

Can I switch plans or get a refund?

Yes. Ask in the app or at hello@prasthana.in and we will move you, pro-rate or refund the current period — a beta should not trap anyone.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free plan needs no payment details at all.

What Yomo is, and is not

Yomo is a read-only money analytics tool built by Prasthana. It cannot move money, place orders, hold funds or act on your accounts, and it never asks for a bank password or an OTP. Yomo is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser and does not sell investment products; its insights are informational, not personalised advice. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks — read the scheme documents before investing.
