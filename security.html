Security FAQ

Asked & Answered Plainly.

Is the sync really read-only?expand_more
Yes — structurally, not by policy alone. Bank data flows through the RBI-regulated Account Aggregator framework which physically cannot transmit payment authority, and broker data uses official Kite Connect read sessions. yomo has no mechanism to withdraw, transfer or execute trades on your behalf.
What exactly does yomo read?expand_more
Only what you consent to: UPI/SMS transaction alerts and statements in your Gmail, holdings and trades via your broker API (e.g. Zerodha Kite), and portfolios consolidated via INDmoney. Parsing is tokenized and credentials are never stored.
How is the data protected?expand_more
AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, ISO 27001-aligned operations, and zero credential storage. Access is consent-scoped, auditable, and revoked the moment you disconnect a source or revoke AA consent in your bank app.
How do I revoke consent?expand_more
Instantly, from your bank's AA consent screen or by disconnecting the source inside yomo. Revocation is effective immediately across the chain.
How do I permanently delete my data?expand_more
Email hello@prasthana.in with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We purge your data and send written confirmation.
Is Yomo giving me investment advice?expand_more
No. Yomo is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser and does not sell investment products. Everything Roast, Pro and Zen produce is analytics and education, not personalized investment advice.
