Security & privacy

Read-only. Encrypted. Yours.

Yomo can read the money that you connect, with your explicit consent. It can never move it, hold it or trade it — there is no code path for that.

Read-only by construction

No payment, transfer or order-placement code exists in the product. Not a permission we promise — a capability we never built.

Encrypted at rest

Alert text and statement payloads are stored encrypted; the database never holds them in plaintext. Traffic runs over TLS.

No products, no commissions

Yomo is not a SEBI-registered adviser and sells nothing. No one pays us to rank a fund, a card or a broker.

Consent you can revoke

Disconnect Gmail, a broker, a forwarding address or the SMS reader inside the app. The feed stops at that moment.

What Yomo actually reads

Gmail bank alerts. Google's read-only OAuth scope, limited to messages from the bank senders you approve. Yomo stores the parsed transaction — amount, date, merchant — not your mailbox.

Statements and CAS. PDFs and CSVs you upload or share: bank statements, credit-card bills and CAMS/KFintech, NSDL or CDSL consolidated statements for mutual funds and demat.

Broker reads. Zerodha through a Kite Connect read session you authorise, plus Groww and INDmoney connections. Holdings and positions are read; orders are never touched.

Bank SMS, on Android only. An allowlist of bank sender headers decides what leaves the phone. Personal messages and OTPs are never read or uploaded.

Delete everything, whenever.

Two taps in Settings: export the ledger as CSV, or delete the account and its data permanently. No retention queue for your transactions, no waiting on support.

Want a human to confirm it?

Write to hello@prasthana.in with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

Request deletion

Nightly backups are kept for 14 days for disaster recovery and then removed; a deletion request also removes the account from the active database immediately.

Security FAQ

Is the sync really read-only?

Yes, structurally. Google's scope is read-only, the broker session is a read session, statements are files you hand over, and the Android reader only forwards bank messages. There is no capability anywhere in Yomo to withdraw, transfer or trade.

Does the language model see my data?

Raw alert text and statement files never reach the model. It receives computed facts — totals, categories, merchant names, dates — and every rupee figure in a reply is produced in Python and checked before it is shown. Account numbers, OTPs and PANs are scrubbed from replies.

How do I stop a feed?

Connections in Settings. Disconnecting Gmail revokes the token, removing a forwarding address stops new mail, and uninstalling the Android app ends the SMS reader. Historical rows stay until you delete them — that is the point of the delete control.

Do you sell my data or show ads?

No. No ads, no data sale, no lead-gen for lenders or card issuers, and no affiliate placement pretending to be a recommendation. You pay for the product; that is the business model.

Is Yomo giving me investment advice?

No. Yomo is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser and does not sell investment products. Roast, Pro and Zen describe your own numbers — analytics and education, not personalised advice.
